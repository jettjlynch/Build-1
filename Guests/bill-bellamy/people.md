## 1. Mark W. Magnusson — founder/owner, Rascals Comedy Club, West Orange NJ

**Who:** Opened Rascals in West Orange around 1984 and ran it for 18 years as
founder and chairman, before taking it public with Edward Rodriguez in 1999.
He personally ran the club through the exact 1990–91 window the dossier's
Ray Romano story is set in.

**Why he'd know something the dossier doesn't have:** Fact #2 in the dossier
(score 8/12, UNVERIFIED) — Bellamy opening for Ray Romano at Rascals around
1990–91 — has **zero venue-side or Romano-side corroboration**; every telling
is Bellamy's own retrospective account. Magnusson, as the club's owner during
that exact period, is the one person positioned to check booking records or
recall the lineup directly — a genuine venue-side check the dossier flags as
missing.

**Reachability:** Not a current public figure with an obvious contact
channel — press coverage of him is all from 2005–2008 (NJBiz profile), and no
current LinkedIn or contact page surfaced in search. Treat as a working
professional who may be findable through the Magnusson Group (his later
business, per search results) rather than a guaranteed-easy contact.

**What to ask:** "Do you have any record — a booking calendar, a lineup
flyer, a memory of your own — of Bill Bellamy opening for Ray Romano at
Rascals around 1990 or 1991, after another act reportedly fell through?"
