## People-finder pass — 2026-09-13

Run against the current dossier.md (through the 2026-09-13 fetch-relay upgrade section). All six were independently confirmed to be real, currently-findable people via WebSearch before being listed (not invented to fill a slot). No outreach drafted or sent for any of them; that's a separate future phase.

- **Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson** — childhood friend from Honolulu, mid-1980s; his own Instagram post is the primary source for the dossier's top-scoring fact (Rock/Honolulu childhood friendship, dossier.md fact #1, currently 9/12 after the Verification-cap correction). **Why he'd know more:** his grandmother Lia Maivia, who ran the wrestling shows the three kids attended together, is deceased — he and Nahnatchka Khan are now the only living witnesses to that specific childhood scene, and his Instagram post is short; he could confirm or add detail beyond what's already been quoted secondhand across wrestling-media aggregators. **Reachability:** a public figure with known institutional contact channels (Seven Bucks Productions, WWE, verified social accounts) — realistically very hard to get a direct response from, not a "just email him" case. **Specific ask:** beyond what his Instagram post already said, does he recall any earlier point of contact between young Nick Khan and wrestling itself — i.e., is there anything connecting those Honolulu childhood visits to Khan's decision, seven years later at UNLV, to take the WrestleMania IX usher job (dossier fact #2)?

- **Freddie Roach** — legendary boxing trainer, owner of Wild Card Boxing Club (Hollywood). Two separate dossier facts run through him: he was Khan's law client in an assault case that Khan got dismissed (dossier fact #5, 9/12), and per his own on-record quote in a Sports Illustrated feature (dossier's verified fact, 7/12), he's the one who "decided to tell Nick Khan" that Pacquiao's promoters may have been shortchanging him. **Why he'd know more:** he's the only named person who can speak first-hand to both ends of Khan's law-to-boxing-world pivot — the actual assault case (what happened, why he chose Khan specifically), and the Vagabond Inn introduction to Pacquiao, which is currently sourced only to Khan's own podcast retellings, not to Roach's side of it. **Reachability:** a highly reachable working professional — runs a public gym with a public "About Us"/contact page and does regular boxing-press and podcast appearances. **Specific ask:** what year, specifically, did the Vagabond Inn introduction to Pacquiao happen, and was Khan already representing him as a lawyer at that point or was it purely social — and can he add anything to his own "I decided to tell Nick Khan" SI.com quote about when and how that conversation actually went?

- **Manny Pacquiao** — global boxing icon, later a Philippine senator. Per dossier fact (9/12), Khan met him — both men pre-fame — in a room at the Vagabond Inn in Hollywood, introduced by Freddie Roach. **Why he'd know more:** he's the only other living witness to that specific meeting besides Khan and Roach, and could confirm or complicate Khan's own retelling (told twice, on two separate podcasts, but never from Pacquiao's side). **Reachability:** a public figure with formal, known channels (political office, MP Promotions, management team) but realistically very hard to reach directly given his current stature — flagged as hard-to-reach, not assumed reachable. **Specific ask:** does he remember that first meeting with Khan the way Khan has described it publicly, and roughly what year did it take place?

- **Evan Dick** — sports/broadcasting agent who moved with Khan from the boutique agency Broder Webb Chervin Silbermann to ICM in mid-2006, named directly in the dossier's single strongest-verified fact (10/12, fetch-confirmed via Deadline). Later followed a similar path to CAA in 2012 (the same move Deadline covered for Khan himself), then left CAA in 2018 to launch his own production/management company, Exit 39, which still shares clients with CAA. **Why he'd know more:** he was literally there for the move the dossier's best-sourced fact describes, and could speak to what Khan was actually doing day-to-day building ICM's Sports Media division in 2005-2006 — a period the dossier currently sources only to two trade write-ups (Deadline, TheWrap), never to a person who was in the room. **Reachability:** a publicly reachable working entertainment-industry professional — has an IMDb page, is quoted in trade press, and runs a named, findable company. **Specific ask:** what was Khan actually like to work under at Broder Webb/ICM in 2005-2006, and does he recall anything about Khan's law-to-agent transition that never made it into the Deadline/TheWrap coverage?

- **Adam Hill** — Las Vegas Review-Journal sports columnist (on staff since 2002; Nevada State Sportswriter of the Year), author of "Las Vegan's journey from WrestleMania 9 usher to WWE president" — the direct-quote source for dossier fact #2 (Khan's own account of ushering WrestleMania IX as an 18-year-old UNLV freshman). **Why he'd know more:** he's the one person who actually interviewed Khan for this story and would have the fuller conversation behind it — including the "Cafe Michelle" detail (Khan reportedly also working full-time at a Las Vegas restaurant while at UNLV) that this dossier's researchers found independently but that wasn't part of the text relayed into the dossier, and the exact verbatim wording of Khan's "$7 an hour" quote, which the dossier flags as not independently re-confirmed by a second source. **Reachability:** a working journalist with a public staff page, Muck Rack profile, and publication contact channel — straightforwardly reachable. **Specific ask:** can he confirm the exact wording of the "$7 an hour" quote as printed, and is there more from that interview about the Cafe Michelle detail or anything else that didn't make the final piece?
