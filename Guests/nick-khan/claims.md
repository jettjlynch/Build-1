## Claim #1 — childhood wrestling-watching, placed in Los Angeles, mid-1980s

**Claim:** In an on-record interview with journalist Ariel Helwani (BT Sport, Aug 2021), Khan said that as a boy of Iranian descent he found "a cultural hero" in The Iron Sheik, and that he and his sister (Nahnatchka Khan) related to the Sheik's verbal barbs against wrestlers like Hulk Hogan. He recalled watching WrestleMania I through III on closed-circuit television **"in Los Angeles, where they were living at the time,"** and separately: *"If you went to public school and grew up in the '80s, you loved wrestling. I watched every Saturday morning. I would try desperately to stay up for 'Saturday Night's Main Event.'"*

**Source:** Covered independently by TheSportster ("9 Things We Learned From Ariel Helwani's Interview With WWE's Nick Khan"), Wrestling Inc ("Facts About WWE Co-CEO Nick Khan Only Hardcore Fans Know"), and Sportskeeda ("6 Major Things WWE President Nick Khan Revealed in His Rare Interview With Ariel Helwani") — three genuinely distinct outlets, not obviously copying each other's exact text (per the assessment already made in `sources.md`'s Monitor-pass entry). A fresh, separately-worded search this pass independently reconfirmed the same account and surfaced the "Saturday Night's Main Event" quote, which wasn't in the text originally captured — a second piece of the same interview surfacing independently, not just a repeat of the same sentence. Search-snippet only; none of the three outlets, nor the original BT Sport interview, has been directly fetched.

- **Specificity: 3** — a named journalist, a named event range (WrestleMania I–III), a specific city, a specific claimed household detail (closed-circuit TV, family viewing), and a vivid personal quote about staying up for a specific show.
- **Verification: 2** (capped) — three separately-worded outlets converge on the same specific claim, which is real corroboration signal, not "one source dressed as many" — but no direct fetch of the original interview or any of the three write-ups has happened, so this stays at the cap rather than 3.
- **Tension flag: YES — vs. `dossier.md` fact #1.** Fact #1 (VERIFIED, Verification=3, the dossier's strongest fact) has Khan, his sister, and Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson together in **Honolulu**, roughly monthly, in the **mid-1980s**, watching Lia Maivia's local wrestling shows. This claim places the Khan family in **Los Angeles** for WrestleMania I–III (March 1985 – March 1987) — squarely the same years. Both could be true across different sub-years of a mobile mid-80s childhood (a family can live in more than one city across a few years), and neither source pins down exact years precisely enough to rule that out — but as it stands, nothing in either account explains a Honolulu-to-LA (or LA-to-Honolulu) move, and this is exactly the kind of specific, checkable tension a guest's own retelling can paper over that a direct question can't.
- **Usable (push-past candidate): YES.** Specificity ≥2 and a genuine, previously-unresolved tension flag.
- **Hard rules:** Pass. Public interview, self-told, about his own family's viewing habits — no private third-party personal-life content.
