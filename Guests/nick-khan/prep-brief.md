## Hero fact + artifact + source

**Hero fact (10/12 — Obscurity 1, Era 3, Specificity 3, Verification 3):** Khan worked WrestleMania IX (Caesars Palace, Las Vegas, April 4, 1993) as an usher — a UNLV freshman who'd just turned 18, he took the job after seeing a posting on a bulletin board in the student union. It paid $7/hour when minimum wage was $4.25. In his own words: *"I was a freshman, and I had just turned 18. [The posting] said you could work the biggest events and get paid for it. They were offering like $7 an hour at a time minimum wage was like $4.25."* He wore a green Caesars Palace polo and, by his own admission, wasn't very good at it. Archival WWE broadcast footage shows him on camera behind Howard Finkel. WrestleMania 41 (2025) returned to Las Vegas for the first time since — with Khan now WWE President.
