## Closing assessment — does anything here clear the hero-fact bar (≥10)?

**Yes, technically — two facts (#1 and #2) land at exactly 10/12.** Both are real, well-corroborated, pass both hard rules, and would be usable as a hero fact under the letter of the rubric.

But I don't think either one is what the rubric is actually reaching for yet, and I want to say that plainly rather than declare victory on M1. Both cleared 10 on the strength of Era + Specificity + Verification while Obscurity sat at 1/3 on both — "in a major profile," not "found via archive, print, or a person." That's the tell. Everything in this dossier came from general web search and a handful of blocked-then-worked-around fetches in one sitting; nothing came from a school paper, a hometown archive dig, or an actual person contacted directly — the three source types the build plan calls out as the ones humans skip and sweepers exist to reach. The Rock/Honolulu story and the WrestleMania IX usher story are both good, but both had already gone around wrestling media in some form before I ever searched for them (Dec 2021 and the WrestleMania 41 press cycle respectively) — I found them, I didn't unearth them.

So: M1 passes its actual test. The rubric works — it correctly separated a 10/12 (Rock/Honolulu) from a 6/12 (the murky father-immigration claim) instead of treating every found fact as equally good, and it caught a bad source before it could reach a brief. But if M2's sweepers turn up hero facts by the same method I just used — general search, capped at Obscurity 1 — the source-type list in the build plan (§2, item 1) isn't being used yet and needs to be. The real test for M2 isn't "does the top fact clear 10," it's "does the top fact clear 10 with Obscurity ≥2" — i.e., did it come from somewhere a generic search wouldn't have found it. Nothing here does that yet.
